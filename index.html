About

Light technology for the modern dairy farm.

White Leaf Technology Group brings frequency LED lighting to dairy farms for milking cows and heifers. Our study site is Dornacker Prairie Acres, where installation started in March of 2025.

The roots of the technology go back to 1993, when Quantum Devices of Barneveld, WI developed an LED for NASA plant growth experiments.

In the barn

Installed over the free stalls.

Primary lighting runs the main feed aisle while the red and blue therapy lighting covers the free stall areas.

Technology

Two light technologies,
operating at different wavelengths.

LEDs are semiconductors that convert electrical energy into light. The color of the light depends on the semiconductor material — and each color does a different job.

Red · longer wavelength

Red light

  • Penetrates directly through an animal’s skin and eyes.
  • Transforms into cellular energy, kick-starting metabolic events — including stimulation of collagen production and an increase in ATP, a boost of energy to the cells.
Blue · shorter wavelength

Blue light

  • Laboratory and clinical studies have validated its “killing power” over a whole range of pathogenic bacteria.
  • Including Staphylococcus aureus, MRSA, Streptococcus, Listeria, Salmonella and E. coli.
