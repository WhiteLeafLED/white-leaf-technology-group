Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we receive the name, farm or company, email, phone number, and message you type in. The form also has a hidden field that people are not asked to fill in. It is only there to filter automated submissions. If you call us, we receive whatever you choose to share. We don’t require an account.

How we use it

Only to answer your question and follow up about your project. Messages go to Dave Klumb at White Leaf Technology Group.

Who else sees it

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. This is a static website served by Cloudflare. Cloudflare may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and operations. Contact form messages are checked with Cloudflare Turnstile and emailed to us through a Cloudflare Worker on our own domain. Videos on this site are files we host. They are not played from YouTube or another video service. Pages load the Manrope typeface from Google Fonts.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies, and it does not include an analytics service. Our own pages do not set cookies. Google Fonts and Cloudflare Turnstile receive the connection data they need to deliver the typeface and to run the contact-form security check.

How long we keep it

We keep messages as long as needed to answer you and keep normal business records. Ask us any time to see, correct, or delete what we have about you.
